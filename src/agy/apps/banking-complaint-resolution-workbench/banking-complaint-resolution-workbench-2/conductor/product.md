# Product Definition - Banking Internal Complaint Tracker

## 1. Vision & Purpose
The Banking Internal Complaint Tracker is a clean, visually appealing web-based dashboard designed to help customer support teams track and resolve customer grievances. By implementing a lightweight workflow, the app provides absolute operational clarity, ensuring that no customer issue falls through the cracks. It provides a visual board representation that is highly intuitive for bank staff.

## 2. Core User Roles
* **Customer Support Agent (Intake Officer):** Receives customer complaints via phone/email, creates new complaint records, and assigns them to investigators.
* **Complaint Specialist (Investigator):** Reviews assigned cases, conducts investigation steps, records findings, and moves the status to resolved.

## 3. Workflow Stages (Simplified Issue Tracking)
To keep the application highly focused and easy to demonstrate, complaints will flow sequentially through four main stages:
1. 🆕 **New:** A newly registered complaint awaiting triage or investigator assignment.
2. 👤 **Assigned:** Allocated to a dedicated Complaint Specialist who has acknowledged the case.
3. ⚙️ **In Progress:** Under active investigation (e.g., verifying transactions, reviewing account history).
4. ✅ **Resolved:** An outcome has been determined, communicated to the customer, and the case is closed.

## 4. Key MVP Features
* **Interactive Kanban Board / Workflow Timeline:** A visually rich board showing complaints grouped by workflow stages. Users can move complaints between stages with modern, drag-and-drop or simple card action buttons.
* **Complaint Registration Form:** A simple form to input customer details (name, email, account type), complaint description, severity (Low, Medium, High), and initial notes.
* **Activity Log / Action Timeline:** A linear history log attached to each complaint card, showing when the status was changed, who changed it, and what comments were left.
* **Visual Dashboard Metrics:** Sleek summary cards at the top of the interface showing total open cases, average resolution times, and counts per stage to keep the application feeling modern and alive.
