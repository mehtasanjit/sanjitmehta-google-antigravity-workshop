# Initial Prompt

Help me design and build an internal Banking Complaint Resolution Workbench for bank case handlers and supervisors. The application should support recording, assigning, investigating, reviewing, approving, and resolving customer complaints while maintaining a clear audit history.

Begin by reading the workspace `AGENTS.md`. Use the installed specification-driven development workflow to help clarify the product requirements, produce a product specification, identify the significant design decisions, and propose an implementation plan before writing application code. Ask for confirmation where required by the workspace guidance.

After the specification and significant design decisions are approved, implement only the agreed initial scope and verify the completed behavior against the approved specification and acceptance criteria. Use only synthetic banking and customer data. Do not add external integrations, production authentication, or agentic features unless they are separately requested and approved. Clearly report anything incomplete or unverified.
