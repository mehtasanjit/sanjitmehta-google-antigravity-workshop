The Done column is getting really long and it's hard to see what we finished recently. Can we add a way to archive cards?

Roughly:

- Any card can be archived from its card. Archived cards disappear from the board and stop counting in the column totals.
- There should be a way to see the archived cards, and to restore one. When I restore a card, it goes back to the bottom of the column it came from.
- I'd also like a single "Archive all" button on the Done column, with a confirmation first, because that's the main use case.
- Search should only look at cards on the board, not archived ones.
- Reset board should clear the archive too.

Please keep the existing card ordering rules intact (no gaps in positions after archiving or restoring) and add tests for the new behaviour.
